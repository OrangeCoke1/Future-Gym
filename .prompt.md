# Task: Rigged body models (Xbot, Michelle) with a panel switch

User uploaded two Mixamo GLBs and asked: "都接上吧,到时候在面板切换".

## Plan
1. `src/render/glbSkin.js`: generic GLB reader for skinned meshes (strides, normalised ints, node hierarchy); merges all
   skinned meshes and skins them into the rest pose in world space; returns joints (names, parents, rest positions).
2. `src/render/riggedModel.js`: scale to our figure's height, hips midpoint at the origin. Per frame, world rotation per
   Mixamo bone from the solved skeleton: pelvis / chest / head frames, spine interpolated, clavicle follows chest→shoulder,
   limbs by shortest turn onto our bone directions (parent twist inherited), foot via our rest foot direction, hand from
   wrist→palm + thumb side, fingers curled per finger. FK positions, soles stood on the skeleton's soles, CPU LBS with
   the file's weights. Own particle body (barycentric surface points, vessels along bones, heart), muscle zones from
   the strongest joint, dumbbell grip point, `mapP()` to put the overlay on the model's joints.
3. Stage: several bodies, `setBody(key)` (lazy load, keep loaded), particles `makeBody` / `use` to swap particle bodies.
4. Hand tracking: per-finger curl (`curlOf`, `HandState.curl`), `sol.fingers`; the stage smooths each finger.
5. Settings `body` (`stick.body`), 面板 → 显示 → 人体模型 原版 / Xbot / Michelle; Mixamo credit in 数据说明; i18n.
6. Checks: node pose test over all actions (timing), lint, build, headless screenshots of both models in all three looks.
