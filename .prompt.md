# Task: Dumbbells stuck in the hands + legs deform + camera window over the whole page + still too jittery

User report: "the dumbbell gets stuck in the hand; leg recognition isn't good, the legs deform every time — can the legs default to a normal pose?"

## Diagnosis

### Dumbbells
1. **Live view:** `camSolve` returns `prop: HAS_PROP[action]`, and `action` is `S.action`. Once a dumbbell exercise
   (curl / press / lateral raise) has been recognised, or picked in the bottom bar, `S.action` stays on it. So the figure keeps holding
   dumbbells while the person squats, walks or stands, until another exercise is recognised.
2. **Replay view:** after `action_end`, `RP.act` stays on the exercise with `RP.q = 0`, so the figure freezes on the last
   scripted frame, still holding dumbbells. Only jog is sent back to standing today.

### Legs
`camSolve` takes the thigh, shin and foot directions straight from the filtered landmarks:
1. **Low-confidence legs are kept.** `put()` accepts a joint at visibility ≥ 0.5, so partly hidden or cropped legs
   (common at a desk or close to the camera) feed noisy guesses. Joints below 0.5 are frozen at whatever value they last had.
2. **No anatomical limits.** MediaPipe's depth (z) for knees and ankles is noisy. The knee can bend backwards (hyperextension), the thigh can
   swing far behind or across the body, and the foot can point anywhere, which looks like the legs "deforming".

## Plan

### 1. Dumbbells (`src/engine/engine.js`, `src/pose/camera.js`)
- `camSolve(cam, action, J, prop)`: take the prop flag from the caller instead of `HAS_PROP[action]`.
- Live view: `prop = !!S.fitAct && HAS_PROP[S.fitAct]`, so dumbbells show only while a dumbbell exercise is actually being
  counted, and disappear on `action_end` (the grip already eases open over ~0.2 s in `stage.js`).
- Replay view: when `RP.q` reaches 0 after `action_end` (`S.fitAct === null`), blend back to standing idle and clear `RP.act`
  for every exercise, not only jog. Phase-synced replay shows dumbbells only while `S.fitAct` is that exercise.

### 2. Legs (`src/pose/camera.js`)
- **Per-joint confidence:** in `camIngest` keep a smoothed visibility per joint (`cam.Vis[name]`, EMA ~0.2 s). Joints below
  0.5 now decay towards 0 confidence instead of being frozen.
- **Leg confidence per side:** `w = clamp((min(vis hip, knee, ankle) − 0.55) / 0.25, 0, 1)`.
- **Neutral leg:** thigh and shin along the body's down axis (`-up`, so legs follow the torso), slightly out, foot along `bodyF`.
  Each side's thigh, shin and foot direction is blended (nlerp) from neutral to tracked by `w`. Fully hidden legs stand normally;
  half-seen legs are mostly normal.
- **Anatomical limits on the tracked directions (before blending):**
  - Knee: if the knee lies behind the hip→ankle line (`dot(offset, bodyF) < 0`), mirror it to the front, so knees never bend backwards.
    Knee flexion is clamped to ≤ 150°.
  - Hip: the thigh direction is expressed in the body frame (side, up, bodyF) and clamped to −30°…+125° front/back and
    −20°…+50° in/out (per side), so legs can't swing behind the body or cross over.
  - Foot: heel→toe direction is kept within 45° of `bodyF` (turned in or out), and falls back to `bodyF` at low confidence.
- `camPredict` doesn't extrapolate low-confidence joints (gain multiplied by confidence).

### 3. Panel option (`CameraTuning.vue`, `camTune.js`, settings store)
- Add a `legs` field to `CamTune`: `'auto'` (default, confidence blend + limits) or `'stand'` (legs always in the neutral standing pose;
  only the upper body follows the person), shown as a two-button segmented control "腿部：自动 / 固定站姿" with ja/en strings.
  Sanitised like the other fields; old saved tunings without it get `'auto'`.

### 4. Camera window over the whole page (`CamPreview.vue`, `useFloatWin.js`, `stage.css`, settings store)
User: "the camera window should move anywhere on the page, not only inside the main view".
- `CamPreview.vue`: render the window with `<Teleport to="body">` and `position: fixed`, so it is no longer clipped by the stage.
- `useFloatWin(el, rect, aspect, commit, place, bounds)`: take the bounding box from an optional `bounds()` callback
  (default: the parent, as now). CamPreview passes the viewport (`innerWidth` × `innerHeight`). Re-clamp on window `resize`
  instead of observing the parent.
- Default place: the stage's bottom-right corner (from `.stage`'s `getBoundingClientRect()`, fallback viewport bottom-right),
  so the window first appears where it does today.
- Stored rect: `stick.camWin` gets a `page: true` marker. Old stage-relative rects without it are dropped and the window is re-placed once.
- `z-index` above the cards and below the panel drawer and its scrim (the drawer still covers it). `stage.css`: drop the `.stage`
  prefix from the video and canvas rule, since the window is no longer inside `.stage`.
- Dragging keeps `stopPropagation`, so it never turns the figure's view.

### 5. Jitter (user: "it still shakes a lot")
Where the shaking comes from, besides the One Euro settings:
- MediaPipe's world-landmark **depth (z)** is far noisier than x / y, but all three axes use the same filter.
- **Low-confidence joints** (often the legs and wrists) jump the most but get the same light smoothing.
- The **body facing** (`side` / `bodyF` from the shoulder line) is re-derived every frame, so shoulder noise turns the whole figure.
- Prediction multiplies residual noise once a joint counts as moving.
Changes:
- Per-axis smoothing: z uses `minCut × 0.4` and `beta × 0.5`.
- Confidence-weighted smoothing: a joint's effective `minCut` scales by `0.4 + 0.6 × confidence`, so shaky, low-confidence joints are smoothed harder
  (together with step 2's blend to the neutral pose).
- Smooth the body frame: low-pass `up` and `side` (≈ 4 Hz, renormalised) in `camSolve` state, plus a 1.5° dead-band on the
  facing so standing still doesn't make the figure turn back and forth.
- Prediction: raise `V_REST` 0.08 → 0.15 m/s, and fade the lead to 0 for joints with confidence < 0.7.
- New default = a steadier "平衡" (minCut 1.5, beta 6, lead 60 ms); 稳定 = (0.8, 3, 0), 跟手 = (3, 12, 120).
- Measure with the offline simulation (noise 1 cm with z noise 3 cm, squat at 0.5 m/s): still-jitter and lag before and after; aim for at least
  half the jitter at under 30 ms of added lag. Numbers go in the PR.

## Not touched
- `fit.js` (counting rules stay upstream's). Counting still uses the raw rules, not the constrained figure.

## Risks
- Real deep squats with knees well forward are kept (only *backward* bends are mirrored); very wide stances beyond 50° are clamped.
- Standing mode makes leg exercises (squat, lunge, high knees) look like upper-body only in the live view. That's why it's opt-in.
